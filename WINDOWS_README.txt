TINY BIGGOT ROGUES @VERSION@: @NAME@ (WINDOWS)

To play:
1. Unzip this folder (right-click it, Extract All).
2. Double-click TinyBiggot.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the game is not signed.
   Press "More info", then "Run anyway". It asks only the first time.

The keys: on the title screen, OPTIONS, then KEYBOARD KEYS shows them and lets you change them.
The mouse: hold the left button to shoot at the cursor, the right button rolls. In every menu,
point at a choice and click it; the wheel moves up and down.
Full screen: F11 (or Alt+Enter) switches between the window and the full screen; it is remembered.
A controller: the left stick moves, the right stick aims and shoots, A uses, B rolls, Y or START is
the menu.
Your progress is saved on this computer, apart from the iPad's.

PLAYING TOGETHER (up to 4 on one screen):
1. Plug in the controllers, then choose CO-OP on the title screen.
2. Each player presses their confirm button to join: Space or Enter on the keyboard (or click a
   hero), A on a controller. Two players can share the keyboard: one joins with Space, the other
   with Enter (see below). Every other player uses a controller.
3. Each player chooses a hero with LEFT and RIGHT (A and D for the left one of two on the
   keyboard) and presses confirm again: READY. The run starts when everyone who joined is
   ready. MENU takes a player out again.
In the run: everyone has their own hearts, gold, items and levels; what you touch is yours. A chest
is for everyone: whoever opens it picks first, then the others in turn from what is left. Foes
are tougher for each extra player. A player who loses all hearts is DOWN: stand by them for 3
seconds to help them up (a cleared room helps everyone up). The doors open for the party when
everyone standing is in the same doorway. Any player's MENU opens their own pause and gear.

TWO ON ONE KEYBOARD (when both have joined with the keyboard):
Left player:  W A S D move. The mouse aims: hold the left button to shoot at it (or let the hero
              shoot at the nearest foe by itself). Space uses and confirms, Left Shift or the
              right mouse button rolls, Q heals, E throws a bomb, F is the special, 1 to 4 the
              belt, Esc or Tab the menu.
Right player: the arrows move. The number pad aims (8, 4, 5, 6) if there is one. Enter uses and
              confirms, Right Shift or number pad 0 rolls, / heals, Right Ctrl or , throws a bomb,
              . is the special, 7 8 9 0 the belt, Backspace the menu.
These keys are fixed. A keyboard player who is the only one on the keyboard keeps every key, as
when playing alone (and the keys chosen in KEYBOARD KEYS).
Many keyboards cannot take a lot of keys held at once: if a key sometimes does nothing while both
players hold several, that is the keyboard, not the game (a gaming keyboard, or a controller for
one of you, does not have this).
