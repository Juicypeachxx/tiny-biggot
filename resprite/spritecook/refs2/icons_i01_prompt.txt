A sprite sheet of 36 different little game item icons in a 6 by 6 grid, redrawn from the reference sheet. Every one stays in its own place in the grid and keeps its design, its parts, its colours and the way it faces, as in the reference. Row 2: a glowing ember; a little spirit familiar; a feather; a frog; a gem; a gourd flask. Row 3: a little hatchet; a red heart; a key; a padlock; an ammo magazine; a red potion. Row 4: a book; a torn page; a chaos alignment badge; an evil alignment badge; a good alignment badge; a law alignment badge. Row 5: a drink: ale; a drink: cider; a drink: fury; a drink: grog; a drink: insight; a drink: mead. Row 6: a drink: smoke; a drink: stout; a drink: whet; a drink: wine; a playing card face down; a playing card face up. The words are only to tell you what each one is: never write any names, words or letters in the picture. Draw each one as pixel art about 20 pixels across, in the hand-made look of the style guide: big square pixels, a bold near-black outline all round, flat bright saturated colours with one shadow tone and one highlight per colour, simple bold readable shapes, no soft shading, no gradients, no noise, no anti-aliasing. Clear empty space between them. No text, no labels, no captions, no background.
